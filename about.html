About the project

Private by design

Encrypt & Decrypt is a free, open-source utility for encrypting and decrypting text using AES-256-GCM. Passwords are processed with PBKDF2-SHA256, using a randomly generated salt and a fresh IV for each encryption.

Encryption and decryption happen on your device. The tool does not require an account or send your messages to a server. It does not intentionally save your text or passwords; content you choose to copy can remain on your device's clipboard.

There is no password recovery service. You are in control of your data and responsible for keeping your password safe. For the full technical and privacy details, see the Privacy Policy and independent decryption guide.

Why I built it

Software without the catches

I believe practical tools should not require an ongoing subscription, intrusive advertising or the collection of personal information. Privacy should be the default, not a paid upgrade.

Encrypt & Decrypt is one way I put that belief into practice: a straightforward tool that does its job, stays out of your way and is free to use.

Transparency

Source you can inspect

The source code is published under GPL v3 so people can study how it works and make changes under the licence terms.

Because it uses standard algorithms, you can also decrypt messages independently without relying on this specific app. The decryption guide explains how.
